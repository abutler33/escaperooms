A little mystery.
A lot of discovery.

Invite your library detectives to solve The Case of the Missing Book through call numbers, book categories, and careful observation.

Launch student activity ↗
BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Set up the case

Open the student activity on each device. Students can work independently or in small teams. Have paper and pencils ready for detective notes.

HOW TO PLAY

Investigate & connect

Explore all ten image tiles. Each opens a larger graphic. Combine evidence to solve four locks in any order. Some information is there to send detectives down a different path.

WRAP UP

Close the case

Opening all four locks reveals the ending. Discuss how call numbers and shelf order help readers find books. Use Reset case to prepare the device for another team.

Teacher note

Progress is saved on this browser when available. Reset the case before sharing a device with a new group. Clues can be opened with the keyboard; Escape closes a clue.
